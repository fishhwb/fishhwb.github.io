Terms of Friendship

Important legal-ish fish notice: this page is a meme, not a binding contract. Real consent, boundaries, privacy, safety and anyone's right to leave or change a friendship always override every joke written below.

By choosing to accept this extremely serious and definitely-not-serious document, the undersigned acknowledges the following friendship lore:

  1. Stupidity is covered. Bad jokes, questionable ideas, cursed experiments and “what happens if we try this?” are expected. Nobody is required to do anything unsafe, illegal or genuinely uncomfortable.
  2. Camera clause. If you're knowingly taking part in a recording, stream or original video with Ded Zed, clips and edits may be made from that recording. Hidden recording is not granted by this page, and reasonable privacy/removal requests still matter.
  3. Flirting may occur. Silly or flirty banter can happen. It stops when someone says no, asks for it to stop or clearly isn't enjoying it. The joke never outranks consent.
  4. The friendship escape hatch has been “removed.” According to the meme, friendship can only advance to Best Friend after enough good times. According to reality, either person can step away whenever they want.
  5. Testing stupid ideas is part of the job description. When summoned, friends are strongly encouraged to appear and help test nonsense when they're free. Availability, safety, common sense and actual responsibilities remain valid excuses.
  6. Best Friend promotion. Promotion may occur after sufficient laughs, chaos, mutual trust, surviving the stupid ideas above and at least one story that sounds fake when retold.
