Terms of Friendship

A completely serious document written by people who should probably not be trusted with completely serious documents.

Actual important bit.This is a joke page, not a legal contract. Consent, boundaries, privacy, safety and anyone's real-world right to leave or change a friendship override every joke below. No checkbox can sign those rights away.
  1. General stupidity coverage.Bad jokes, cursed ideas, impulsive experiments and sentences beginning with “hear me out” are considered normal operating conditions. Unsafe, illegal or genuinely uncomfortable ideas remain optional in the sense that the correct option is “no.”
  2. The stupid-idea testing department.Friends may be summoned to test strange software, VRChat nonsense, questionable game mechanics, broken prototypes and other things that probably worked five minutes ago. Attendance is strongly encouraged by the fictional management and completely optional in reality.
  3. Recording and clipping clause.If you knowingly appear in a stream, recording or original video with Ded Zed, funny moments may be clipped, edited or reused from that recording. This does not grant permission for hidden recording, private conversations or ignoring a reasonable request not to use something sensitive.
  4. Flirting may happen.Flirty banter, compliments and jokes may appear without warning because apparently professionalism was never installed. If somebody says stop, seems uncomfortable or simply is not into it, the flirting stops. Easy.
  5. The friendship cancellation button is allegedly missing.According to company lore, ordinary friendship cannot be cancelled and the only available upgrade path is Best Friend. According to reality, either person can step away whenever they want because this is a meme and not a hostage situation.
  6. Best Friend promotion requirements.Promotion may be awarded after enough laughs, trust, mutual chaos, late-night nonsense and at least one story that sounds completely fabricated despite being true. No interview is required. HR has been replaced by a fish.
  7. Banter and friendly roasting licence.Light roasting is permitted when everybody is enjoying it. The moment a joke actually hurts, the comedian has accidentally left the comedy department and must correct course.
  8. Disappearing for a bit is legal.Friends are allowed to sleep, work, recharge, become distracted for three business centuries or simply not feel social. A delayed reply is not automatically a declaration of war.
  9. Confidential nonsense stays confidential.Personal information, private vents, embarrassing stories and things clearly shared in confidence do not become public material merely because this page contains a recording clause.
  10. Emergency “I told you so” rights.If one friend warns the other that an obviously stupid plan will fail and the plan then fails spectacularly, one ceremonial “I told you so” is permitted. Excessive use may result in objects being thrown in VR.
  11. Snack taxation.Snacks voluntarily placed within friendship range may be subject to a completely fictional friendship tax. Actual theft remains, inconveniently, theft.
  12. VR and game chaos clause.Launching friends into the sky, trapping them in badly tested mechanics, making them inspect cursed avatars or asking them to stand somewhere “for science” is acceptable only while everyone involved is having fun.
  13. No warranty on judgement.Friendship is supplied “as is.” No guarantee is made that either party will make sensible choices, remember why they entered a room, understand the code they wrote yesterday or resist pressing the clearly labelled bad button.
  14. Bug-report formatting.Reports such as “it broke,” “your thing exploded,” and “I touched nothing” are technically accepted. Screenshots and an explanation of what happened remain annoyingly useful.
  15. Friendship patch notes.These terms may gain additional nonsense whenever a new recurring joke deserves paperwork. Real boundaries do not change unless the actual people involved agree to them.
  16. Final clause.By reaching clause sixteen you have demonstrated either commitment to the friendship or an alarming willingness to read terms nobody reads. Both are appreciated.
