# Google AdSense setup for FISH HWB

The new `bait/` page is intentionally a **parody ad wall**. Do not place Google AdSense units on that page and do not replace its fake cards with live Google ads.

Google's policies prohibit deceptive ad placement, accidental-click layouts, ads on pages made mainly to show ads, and obstructive popup-style implementations. The fake startup card and the parody wall are therefore kept completely separate from any future AdSense integration.

## Recommended setup

1. Sign in to Google AdSense and add `fishhwb.github.io` under **Sites**.
2. In AdSense, open **Ads** and choose **Get code** for Auto ads, or create a normal display ad unit under **By ad unit**.
3. Copy the exact code Google gives you. For Auto ads, place the AdSense loader inside the `<head>` of normal content pages such as `index.html`.
4. Use normal in-page placements with enough spacing from navigation, buttons and interactive content. Do not label Google ads as jokes, downloads, navigation or "Hot Single Fish".
5. Exclude `/bait/` from Auto ads in the AdSense Auto ads settings.
6. If Google asks you to publish an `ads.txt`, put the exact line supplied by your AdSense account in `/ads.txt` at the repository root.

A typical loader will look structurally like this, but use the exact publisher ID and code generated by your own account:

```html
<script async
  src="https://pagead2.googlesyndication.com/pagead/js/adsbygoogle.js?client=ca-pub-YOUR_PUBLISHER_ID"
  crossorigin="anonymous"></script>
```

## UK / EEA / Switzerland consent

For personalised ads served to users in the UK, EEA or Switzerland, Google requires a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. Set that up before enabling personalised ads for those visitors.

## Suggested places on this site

Good candidates are normal content sections where an ad will not be confused with controls: between major homepage sections, below Videos, or near the footer. Keep the site content dominant over paid advertising.

Avoid putting live Google ads inside the startup parody modal, on the `/bait/` parody wall, directly beside the close button, over the Twitch/YouTube players, or anywhere a user could click one accidentally while trying to use the site.
